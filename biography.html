His Biography

The story of a remarkable Kenyan leader

Early Life

Raila Amolo Odinga was born on 7 January 1945 in Maseno, Kenya. He was the son of Jaramogi Oginga Odinga, a prominent Kenyan nationalist and the country's first Vice President.

Education

Raila pursued education in Germany, where he studied engineering and developed a strong academic foundation.

Political Journey

His political journey was marked by activism, challenges, imprisonment, political struggles, and a continued commitment to democratic reform.

Leadership

Throughout his career, he became one of Kenya's most influential political figures, playing significant roles in the country's democratic development and national conversations.

His story remains part of Kenya's history.
